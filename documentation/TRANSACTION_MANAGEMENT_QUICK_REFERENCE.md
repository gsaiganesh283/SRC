## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use Search First**
   - Faster than scrolling
   - Narrows results quickly

2. **Filter + Search**
   - Combine for precision
   - Example: Year 2024 + "John"

3. **Export Before Delete**
   - Safety backup
   - Can't undo deletes

4. **Verify Regularly**
   - Don't let it pile up
   - Easier to track

5. **Use Pagination**
   - 50 records = sweet spot
   - Faster page loads

6. **Keyboard Shortcuts**
   - Ctrl+E for quick export
   - Ctrl+B for bulk mode
   - Esc to cancel
